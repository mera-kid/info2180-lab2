About

Hey there! My name is Maya Ellis and I am a student at the University of the West Indies, pursuing a major in Computer Science and a minor in Digital Media Production. I enjoy creating things, from art and music, to programs and games. I believe that if I am able to make even one person happier with something I have made, I would have done something tremendously worthwhile.
